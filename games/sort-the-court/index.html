About this game

Give your royal decree in quick yes-or-no calls and balance gold, people and happiness as your kingdom grows.

Subjects queue up with requests and you have seconds to say yes or no. Every decree nudges your gold, population and happiness — let any one of them collapse and the kingdom suffers. It opens on the developer's itch.io page.
