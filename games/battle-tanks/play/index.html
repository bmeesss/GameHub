Battle Tanks

Two tanks, destructible crates and bouncing shells. Player 1 steers with WASD and fires with F, player 2 uses the arrow keys and Enter — or let the AI take tank two.

P1: W/A/S/D + F · P2: arrows + Enter · M switches to AI · P pauses
