Farm Defender

Critters march from the hedge to the barn. Buy turrets with coins and place them on the grass — every turret added also makes the next placements pricier, so plan the field.

Pick a turret with 1/2/3 or the buttons · click grass to build · critters reach the barn and you lose a life
